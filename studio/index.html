Click any post to open the editor: click text to rewrite it, set the background and layers from the right panel, drag slides to reorder a carousel, switch the post size, then export PNG, PDF (carousel) or a subtle 6-second video. Esc closes.

Hover any block (a line of copy, a product illustration, the logo) and click its ✕ to remove it. Reset to original in the right panel restores the post exactly as it was designed.

On the grid, hover a card for its number, ↓ PNG, ♥ favourite, ⧉ duplicate and ✕ remove — favourites and edits are saved automatically in this browser.

This studio is a free tool you can own

It is a plain folder of web pages: no accounts, no server, no install. Everything runs in your browser and your edits stay on your computer.

1Get your own copy. On the GitHub page, click the green Code button, then Download ZIP (or fork it if you use GitHub). Unzip anywhere, then double-click start-mac.command on a Mac or start-windows.bat on Windows. Full details in the README.
2Connect it to Claude Code. Open the folder in Claude Code and just ask: "set this up for my brand, here is our website". The included AGENTS.md teaches Claude how the tool works, so it can build your brand, write posts, and generate product illustrations for you.
3Change the tool itself, any time. Nothing here is fixed: it is all plain code that Claude knows how to edit. Ask for a new layout, a new export format, a whole new feature, and the tool reshapes itself around you.
