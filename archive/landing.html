Make it yours
1Copy the demo folder

Duplicate brands/demo and rename it, e.g. brands/acmecorp. Drop your logo and photos into its assets/ folder.

2Fill in your brand

Open studio.mjs in the new folder. It walks you through it: colors, fonts, logo, backgrounds, and your starter posts.

3Register it

Add the folder name to brands/registry.mjs, then open studio/?v=acmecorp. Full guide: CUSTOMIZE.md.

Faster route: open this folder in Claude Code and say "set this studio up for my brand, here is our website". The included AGENTS.md tells Claude exactly how this tool works.
